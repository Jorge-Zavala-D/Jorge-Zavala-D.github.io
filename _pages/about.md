      <p class="section-kicker">Research agenda</p>
      <h2>Breaking cycles of violence</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="measure">
      <p>
        My dissertation asks how political violence travels from institutions and communities
        into households, beliefs, and life trajectories—and how material and informational forms
        of transitional justice can change those pathways.
      </p>
      <p>
        The agenda connects political economy, development economics, and comparative politics.
        It is organized around post-conflict Peru and speaks to wider questions about recognition,
        legitimacy, migration, and the reach of the state.
      </p>
    </div>
  </section>
