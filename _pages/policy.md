<section class="policy-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <div>
      <p class="section-kicker">Selected applied research</p>
      <h2>From field systems to policy decisions</h2>
    </div>
    <p class="section-aside">
      I lead quantitative research from survey design and data-quality systems through causal or
      diagnostic analysis, reproducible delivery, and policy communication.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="applied-grid">
    <article>
      <div class="work-card__meta"><span>Migration</span><span>Latin America &amp; the Caribbean</span></div>
      <h3>Unlocking migration's economic contribution</h3>
      <p class="applied-grid__client">IOM · Equilibrium SDC · 2025</p>
      <p>
        Comparative macro- and microeconomic analysis of migrants' labor-market and fiscal
        contributions across the region, combined with qualitative evidence on the institutional
        barriers that prevent human capital from translating into formal employment and higher
        productivity.
      </p>
      <p class="project-facts">Role: quantitative consultant and contributing author</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <div class="work-card__meta"><span>Financial inclusion</span><span>Colombia</span></div>
      <h3>Financial inclusion for NARP and Indigenous communities</h3>
      <p class="applied-grid__client">IFC / IDB Invest · Fundación Capital &amp; Equilibrium SDC · 2024–25</p>
      <p>
        A mixed-methods market study combining 1,000 surveys across five municipalities,
        unsupervised segmentation, interviews, and focus groups to identify barriers, client
        profiles, and viable opportunities for more inclusive financial products.
      </p>
      <p class="project-facts">Methods: survey research · K-means · mixed-methods segmentation</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <div class="work-card__meta"><span>Public health</span><span>Peru</span></div>
      <h3>Migrant access to health and social protection</h3>
      <p class="applied-grid__client">WHO / PAHO · Equilibrium SDC · 2025</p>
      <p>
        Quantitative systems for field monitoring, survey quality, reproducible data preparation,
        and segmentation to understand migrant experiences with access, discrimination, and
        service delivery.
      </p>
      <p class="project-facts">Methods: field-data QA · reproducible analysis · clustering</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <div class="work-card__meta"><span>Migration attitudes</span><span>Peru</span></div>
      <h3>Public perceptions of Venezuelan migration</h3>
      <p class="applied-grid__client">CEDRO · Equilibrium SDC · 2024</p>
      <p>
        Baseline survey research on intergroup contact, perceived insecurity, economic beliefs,
        social distance, and media exposure in four districts of metropolitan Lima.
      </p>
      <p class="project-facts">Methods: instrument design · field monitoring · data quality</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <div class="work-card__meta"><span>Urban services</span><span>Paraguay</span></div>
      <h3>Sanitation access in Fernando de la Mora</h3>
      <p class="applied-grid__client">Inter-American Development Bank · Equilibrium SDC · 2023–25</p>
      <p>
        Baseline, midline, and endline survey systems for an urban sanitation program, including
        geospatial sampling, real-time field audits, panel reconciliation, and measurement of
        neighborhood spillovers.
      </p>
      <p class="project-facts">Methods: longitudinal surveys · geospatial sampling · spillover measurement</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <div class="work-card__meta"><span>Disability inclusion</span><span>Multi-country</span></div>
      <h3>Barriers to healthcare for people with intellectual disabilities</h3>
      <p class="applied-grid__client">Special Olympics · Equilibrium SDC</p>
      <p>
        Multi-country analysis of access, affordability, communication barriers, and perceived
        quality of care using survey harmonization, index construction, regression, and
        segmentation.
      </p>
      <p class="project-facts">Methods: harmonization · index construction · regression · clustering</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
