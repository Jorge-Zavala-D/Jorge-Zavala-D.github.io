This site uses GoatCounter to understand how many people visit and how they find the site. Analytics are optional and load only if you accept them. You can use the entire site, including both CV downloads, without accepting.

GoatCounter receives the page visited and the referring address when your browser supplies one. It can derive broad location and browser or device statistics from the request and stores aggregate statistics rather than an individual browsing history. I do not use advertising trackers or record clicks on individual downloads. See [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for details.

The site saves your analytics choice in your browser. To change it or stop future tracking, open the settings below and reject analytics. If you have questions about this site, email [Jorge Zavala](mailto:jorge.zavala@wzb.eu).
