Selected applied research

From field systems to policy decisions

I lead quantitative research from survey design and data-quality systems through causal or diagnostic analysis, reproducible delivery, and policy communication.

MigrationLatin America & the Caribbean

Unlocking migration's economic contribution

IOM · Equilibrium SDC · 2025

Comparative macro- and microeconomic analysis of migrants' labor-market and fiscal contributions across the region, combined with qualitative evidence on the institutional barriers that prevent human capital from translating into formal employment and higher productivity.

Role: quantitative consultant and contributing author

Financial inclusionColombia

Financial inclusion for NARP and Indigenous communities

IFC / IDB Invest · Fundación Capital & Equilibrium SDC · 2024–25

A mixed-methods market study combining 1,000 surveys across five municipalities, unsupervised segmentation, interviews, and focus groups to identify barriers, client profiles, and viable opportunities for more inclusive financial products.

Methods: survey research · K-means · mixed-methods segmentation

Public healthPeru

Migrant access to health and social protection

WHO / PAHO · Equilibrium SDC · 2025

Quantitative systems for field monitoring, survey quality, reproducible data preparation, and segmentation to understand migrant experiences with access, discrimination, and service delivery.

Methods: field-data QA · reproducible analysis · clustering

Migration attitudesPeru

Public perceptions of Venezuelan migration

CEDRO · Equilibrium SDC · 2024

Baseline survey research on intergroup contact, perceived insecurity, economic beliefs, social distance, and media exposure in four districts of metropolitan Lima.

Methods: instrument design · field monitoring · data quality

Urban servicesParaguay

Sanitation access in Fernando de la Mora

Inter-American Development Bank · Equilibrium SDC · 2023–25

Baseline, midline, and endline survey systems for an urban sanitation program, including geospatial sampling, real-time field audits, panel reconciliation, and measurement of neighborhood spillovers.

Methods: longitudinal surveys · geospatial sampling · spillover measurement

Disability inclusionMulti-country

Barriers to healthcare for people with intellectual disabilities

Special Olympics · Equilibrium SDC

Multi-country analysis of access, affordability, communication barriers, and perceived quality of care using survey harmonization, index construction, regression, and segmentation.

Methods: harmonization · index construction · regression · clustering
