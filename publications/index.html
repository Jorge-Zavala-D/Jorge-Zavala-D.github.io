  1. The Myth of the Misinformed Migrant? Survey Insights from Nigeria’s Irregular Migration Epicenter
    Bernd Beber, Alexandra Scacco, and Jorge E. Zavala
    Revise and resubmit , 2026
    Revise and resubmit

  2. Are Collective Reparations Transitional or Transformational? Migration and Policy Feedback in Post-Conflict Peru
    Matthew Bird, Ben Dumez, and Jorge E. Zavala
    Forthcoming 2026 , 2026
    Forthcoming
    Abs

    Using a fuzzy regression discontinuity design, this study evaluates whether Peru’s Collective Reparations Program strengthened state–society relations and access to public programs while also transforming community trajectories through out-migration.

  3. Recognition-through-Information? Transitional Justice Knowledge, Legitimacy, and Rule-of-Law Orientations in Post-Conflict Peru
    Jorge E. Zavala
    Pilot implemented; manuscript in progress , 2026
    Working paper
    Abs

    A cluster-randomized saturation pilot in rural Ayacucho and San Martín studies whether neutral information about Peru’s transitional-justice architecture changes knowledge, perceived recognition, institutional legitimacy, rule-of-law orientations, and civic efficacy.

  4. Historical Conflict Exposure and the Intergenerational Transmission of Violence: Evidence from ENARES 2024 and Peru’s Internal Armed Conflict
    Jorge E. Zavala
    Dissertation chapter , 2026
    Research in progress
    Abs

    This study links historical conflict exposure with contemporary victimization, tolerance of coercion, and violent socialization in households and schools in Peru.

  5. Digital Bridges for Financial Inclusion: The Role of Public Digital Infrastructure in Empowering Migrant Women and Facilitating Remittances in Colombia
    Angélica Cardozo and Jorge E. Zavala
    Supported by the Center for Financial Inclusion , 2026
    Working paper
    Abs

    A mixed-methods study of how Venezuelan migrant women navigate identity, payments, remittances, trust, fraud, and recourse in Colombia’s evolving digital public infrastructure ecosystem.

  6. Coaching the Match: Experimental Evidence from Chatbot Assistance and AI Augmentation in Mozambique’s Gig Economy
    Jorge E. Zavala and Carla Srebot
    Planned sample: 1,200 workers , 2026
    Pre-analysis plan and working paper in progress
    Abs

    A three-arm randomized evaluation tests whether WhatsApp-delivered business micro-learning and AI-enabled tools improve job volume, completion, earnings proxies, professionalization, and resilience among informal service workers using Mozambique’s Biscate platform.

  7. Evaluating CBT Group Interventions in Conflict-Affected Nigerian Communities
    Nora Chirikure, Macartan Humphreys, Madalena Kresimon, Alexandra Scacco, and Jorge E. Zavala
    WZB, GIZ, and IOM , 2026
    Randomized evaluation in progress
    Abs

    A two-wave randomized evaluation across 24 conflict-affected communities in Kaduna, Plateau, and Taraba studies whether group-based cognitive behavioral therapy improves psychological recovery, daily functioning, social connection, and verified community participation.
