04

Current Research Projects

4 entries

2025 –Present

Breaking Cycles of Violence

Cumulative dissertation on the intergenerational legacies of violence and the material and informational pathways through which transitional justice can generate recognition, legitimacy, and durable repair.

  • Historical conflict exposure and the intergenerational transmission of violence in Peru.
  • Collective reparations, migration, and policy feedback in post-conflict Peru.
2026 –Present

Evaluating CBT Group Interventions in Conflict-Affected Nigerian Communities

Two-wave randomized evaluation across 24 communities in Kaduna, Plateau, and Taraba.

  • Coauthors: Nora Chirikure, Macartan Humphreys, Madalena Kresimon, and Alexandra Scacco.
  • Outcomes: psychological recovery, functioning, agency, social connection, and verified community participation.
2025 –Present

Digital Bridges for Financial Inclusion

Mixed-methods research on digital public infrastructure, remittances, fraud, financial autonomy, and Venezuelan migrant women in Colombia.

  • Coauthor: Angélica Cardozo.
  • Combines surveys, latent class analysis, interviews, focus groups, institutional interviews, and regional benchmarking.
2025 –Present

Coaching the Match

Three-arm randomized evaluation of WhatsApp micro-learning and AI-enabled business tools for informal service providers using the Biscate platform in Mozambique.

  • Coauthor: Carla Srebot.
  • Planned sample: 1,200 active workers.
